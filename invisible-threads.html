About the Author

Avinash Kumar writes psychological and conceptual fiction about the space between what the mind experiences and what it can explain — consciousness, perception, memory, identity, and the strange territory where psychology meets mystery.

What This Book Is About

Ten standalone case files, each unpacking a different hidden mechanism of the mind — the moment before a decision, the confidence behind a false memory, the pull of a stranger's trust. Written for readers who want their fiction to leave them thinking differently about their own mind, not just entertained for an evening.

  • Ten self-contained psychological case studies, woven into one throughline
  • Grounded in real cognitive science, dramatized as fiction
  • For readers of conceptual thrillers who want ideas as much as suspense
