Why Does a Good Person Go Quiet When Something Is Wrong?

Most of us believe we would speak up. Yet in the moment, people often stay silent, scanning others for a cue that never comes. When everyone waits, no one acts. Silence is not always agreement or cowardice. Sometimes it is ordinary people, each hoping someone else will go first.
