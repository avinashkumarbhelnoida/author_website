Avinash Kumar

A keen observer of the visible and unseen, the different facets of human behaviour, fascinated by the hidden mechanisms of the mind—how they influence the way we think, perceive, choose, remember, and ultimately construct the reality we believe we know. Avinash Kumar writes psychological and conceptual fiction about the space between what the mind experiences and what it can explain.
