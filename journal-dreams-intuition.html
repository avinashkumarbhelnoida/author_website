You wake from a vivid dream, certain you will remember it. Ten minutes later, only a scrap is left. Hours of rich, strange experience, gone. The brain builds these worlds every night and then lets most of them dissolve. What is it protecting you from, or hiding?

Why Does Your Mind Rehearse Disasters While You Sleep?

Falling, being chased, showing up unprepared. Dreams love a crisis. One idea is that the mind uses the quiet of sleep to practise threats you cannot safely practise awake. If so, your nightmares might be training sessions: uncomfortable, but strangely protective.

How Do Experts “Just Know” Without Knowing How?

A firefighter senses a floor is about to give way. A chess master glances at the board and sees the best move. Ask them how, and they shrug. Years of experience compress into a feeling that arrives before the reasoning. Intuition is not magic. It is expertise moving faster than words.
